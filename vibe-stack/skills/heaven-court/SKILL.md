# Heaven Court (Pillar III Courtroom Ops)

Division of labor: the `court-of-heaven-project` Knowledge topic owns the
case files and framework; `experiment-smith` owns artifact attack
batteries; `challenge-my-thinking` (first-party, dispatcher-bound) owns
on-demand adversarial reasoning. THIS skill owns adjudication
PROCEDURE: how a claim, position, or verdict gets tried.

## Session procedure

1. **Open the session**: name the case, the parties (proposition vs
   opposition), the claims under trial, and the stakes + penalty for a
   wrong verdict. Stakes stated BEFORE testimony begins (shadow-game
   honesty, Pillar I).
2. **Take testimony**: every load-bearing claim restated as numbered
   statements the court can address one by one.
3. **Press each statement** ("Hold it!"): at least one follow-up probe
   per load-bearing statement before proceeding — force specificity
   until the statement is either precise or exposed as vague.
4. **Present evidence** ("Objection!"): run the discriminating test or
   fetch the authoritative source; the objection names the statement
   number, the evidence, and the contradiction in the same breath.
   An objection without evidence attached is not an objection.
5. **Penalty honesty**: an unfounded objection costs a penalty
   (credibility debt), recorded in the trial record — never buried,
   never quietly retried.
6. **Turnabout**: when an attack flips the case, stop, name the
   turnabout, chase the root cause it revealed (never patch the
   symptom), restate the verdict conditions, resume trial.
7. **Verdict**: lands only on proof-of-carry (engine rule 19) —
   enumerate exactly which evidence survived to the end, in the
   verdict itself. "I'm confident" is not a verdict (rule 22). The
   trial record IS the receipt.

## Standing rules

- The courtroom is for claims, not hearts: solemn or personal matters
  leave the courtroom frame entirely (matrix solemnity override).
- Real-human harm exits the fiction immediately and escalates to real
  humans; the court never claims clinical, legal, or crisis authority.
- Mode B and external deliverables: courtroom terms translate to plain
  verification language (statement, probe, evidence, finding).
- A trial that ends without a verdict or with an untested load-bearing
  claim is adjourned honestly, never declared won.
